# ARC-045 — Launch a usable catalogue and representative history to members

**Depends on:** [ARC-044](ARC-044-restricted-production-pilot.md).

## Outcome

Members receive access to the reviewed digital catalogue and a representative
set of linked historical concerts that is already more useful than Drive.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Run the selected one-time migration through the proven workflow, review
  clear/ambiguous results and resolve affected duplicates before publication.
- [ ] Publish the usable digital catalogue with explicit optional-metadata gaps;
  record reviewed/imported totals rather than treating 500 as an exact requirement.
- [ ] Complete selected concerts across different years with source evidence,
  correct arrangements where known and useful recording passages.
- [ ] Invite the agreed current members/musical leadership and provide concise
  German usage guidance and a contact path to the editor team.
- [ ] Record the remaining 30-year recording/120-year historical enrichment
  backlog, current cost position and maintainer/editor ownership.

## Verification

Have a member complete the find-material and historical-recording journeys on
real reviewed content. Reconcile migration summaries, publication visibility and
invitation outcomes. Full historical indexing is not a condition of completion.

## Handoff and parallel work

After the technical gate, editors can review independent folder/event batches
concurrently using the application's own consistency controls. Follow-up issues
remain a separately enabled phase, not launch blockers.
